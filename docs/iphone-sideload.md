# Try TrailMind on your iPhone (free)

TrailMind for iPhone isn't on the App Store yet. You can install the preview yourself, free, with your own Apple ID
and a free tool on a Windows PC or Mac. The first time takes about 15 minutes.

You need:

- an iPhone with iOS 17 or later (iPhone Duo included) and its cable
- a Windows PC or a Mac
- the TrailMind `.ipa` file you were sent (for example `TrailMind-0.2.0.ipa`)
- your Apple ID. The tool signs in to Apple with it to sign TrailMind for your iPhone only; some people use a spare
  Apple ID for this.

## Install with Sideloadly

1. Download Sideloadly from its official site, [sideloadly.io](https://sideloadly.io), and install it. On Windows,
   follow the site's note about the Apple software it needs.
2. Plug in your iPhone, unlock it, and tap **Trust** if it asks.
3. Open Sideloadly, choose your iPhone, and drag the `.ipa` file onto the window.
4. Enter your Apple ID. Open **Advanced options** and set **Bundle ID** to `com.trailmind.ios.beta.` followed by
   your first name in lower-case letters, for example `com.trailmind.ios.beta.sam`. Keep the same one for every
   update, so your routes and hikes stay.
5. Click **Start**, and enter the code Apple sends if it asks.
6. On the iPhone: **Settings → General → VPN & Device Management**, tap your Apple ID under Developer App, then
   **Trust**.
7. **Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode**: turn it on. The iPhone restarts; tap **Turn On** when asked.
   (The switch appears once TrailMind is installed.)
8. Open TrailMind.

## Every 7 days

Apps signed with a free Apple ID stop opening after 7 days. Plug the iPhone in, open Sideloadly and press **Start**
again with the same Bundle ID: TrailMind is signed again and your routes and hikes are kept.
[AltStore](https://altstore.io) and [SideStore](https://sidestore.io) can instead refresh it from the iPhone itself
after a one-time setup with the computer; their sites have guides.

## Updates

When you're sent a new `.ipa`, install it the same way, with the same Bundle ID.

## Good to know

- OS maps need your own free OS Data Hub API key: get one at [osdatahub.os.uk](https://osdatahub.os.uk) and enter
  it in TrailMind's Settings. OpenStreetMap works without one.
- It's a preview: don't rely on it alone for navigation or safety.
- A free Apple ID can have up to three apps installed this way at once.
- TrailMind sends nothing to its developer. Please send feedback and screenshots by message or email.
- On iPhone Duo, please try it closed (on the cover screen), open in landscape and portrait, half-folded like a book,
  and standing like a laptop (tabletop), and say what looked wrong.
